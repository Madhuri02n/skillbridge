# SkillBridge AI — Marketing Plan

## 1. Positioning & messaging

**Positioning statement:**
For job seekers who keep getting rejected without explanation, SkillBridge AI is the
resume-to-JD analysis tool that tells you exactly which skills you're missing and gives
you a 2-week plan to close the gap — unlike generic resume checkers that only optimize
for keywords.

**Core message:** *Stop guessing why you didn't get the interview.*

**Supporting pillars:**
1. **Honest, not flattering** — real score, real gaps.
2. **Actionable, not just diagnostic** — a roadmap, not a verdict.
3. **Fast** — paste and go, results in seconds.

## 2. Landing-page copy

**Hero headline:** Know exactly why you're not getting the interview.

**Subheadline:** SkillBridge AI compares your resume against any job description in
seconds — scoring your fit, naming your gaps, and handing you a 2-week roadmap to close
them.

**CTA button:** Analyze my resume — free

**Section — How it works:**
1. Paste your resume and a job description.
2. Get an instant AI-powered fit score and skill breakdown.
3. Follow a personalized 2-week roadmap to close the gap.

**Section — For placement cells:** Give every student a personal skill-gap report against
real job postings — at a fraction of the cost of 1:1 mentoring.

**Trust line:** No résumé stored without your permission. No spam. Just clarity.

The problem usually isn't your resume's design — it's a handful of specific skills the
JD wants that your resume doesn't show. I built a tool that tells you exactly which ones,
in seconds. Link in comments.

I'm a fresher. I've read more job descriptions this year than I can count, and rejected

focus on interview prep instead of gap-spotting.

**Post 5 — Social proof / CTA (LinkedIn):**

## 4. Promotional creative / video concept

**30-second concept — "The Silent Rejection":**
- 0-5s: Split screen — a candidate hits "Submit Application" confidently; cut to an
  automated rejection email landing seconds later. No explanation shown.
- 5-15s: Candidate pastes resume + JD into SkillBridge AI. Screen recording shows the
  match score animating in, missing skills appearing as tags.
- 15-25s: Quick montage of the 2-week roadmap unfolding — checklist items ticking off.
- 25-30s: End card — "Stop guessing. Know your gap. SkillBridge AI." + URL.

Tone: fast, confident, slightly wry about the pain of job hunting — not somber.

## 7. Follow-up approach

- **Day 0:** Initial outreach (email or LinkedIn/WhatsApp as above).
- **Day 3 (no response):** Short bump — "Just floating this back up — happy to do a quick
  live demo instead if that's easier than reading through it."
- **Day 7 (no response):** Value-add follow-up — share one concrete stat or a sample
  report screenshot instead of asking again; lower-pressure, higher-value nudge.
- **Day 14:** Final follow-up — "Totally understand if the timing's off — happy to
  reconnect whenever a pilot batch makes sense. Leaving this here for when it's useful."
- **On any reply:** Move immediately to scheduling a 15-minute walkthrough call; don't
  let warm replies sit.
