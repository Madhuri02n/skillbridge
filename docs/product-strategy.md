# SkillBridge AI — Product & Business Strategy

## 1. Problem

Every year, millions of job seekers in India (freshers, career-switchers, bootcamp
graduates) apply to dozens of roles and get rejected with no feedback. They don't know
whether they were rejected for a missing skill, a wrong keyword, or a mismatched
experience level — so they can't fix anything before the next application.

On the other side, college placement cells (TPOs) and bootcamp career-services teams are
expected to give hundreds of students individualized feedback with almost no tooling —
usually a single overworked coordinator eyeballing resumes in Excel.

Generic "resume checker" tools (Jobscan, Resume Worded, etc.) mostly optimize for ATS
keyword density. They don't tell a candidate *what to actually go learn* in order to
close a real skill gap for a real job.

## 2. Target users

| Segment | Who | Pain |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Individual job seekers — freshers, career switchers, final-year students | Rejected with no explanation; don't know what to study next |
| Secondary | College placement cells (TPOs) / training & placement officers | Can't give personalized feedback to hundreds of students manually |
| Secondary | Bootcamps & ed-tech career-services teams | Need to prove placement outcomes; want scalable career coaching |
| Tertiary | Independent career coaches / resume writers | Want an AI co-pilot to speed up client sessions |

## 3. Why they'd use / pay for it

- **Individuals:** Free/low tier gets a fast, honest answer instead of hours of guessing
  and rewriting. A ₹99–₹299 one-time or monthly plan for unlimited analyses is cheap
  compared to a resume-writing service (₹1,500–₹5,000) or a career coaching session.
- **Placement cells / bootcamps:** Pay per-seat or per-batch (e.g. ₹5,000–₹20,000/batch of
  50-100 students) to replace hours of manual 1:1 review with instant, consistent,
  data-backed reports — and to show measurable "readiness scores" to management.
- **Career coaches:** Pay a small monthly SaaS fee to generate a data-backed starting
  point for every client conversation instead of starting from scratch.

## 4. What makes it different

- **Honest, not flattering.** It states a real match score and real gaps instead of
  inflating confidence — closer to a hiring manager's actual view than a keyword-stuffing
  tool.
- **Actionable, not just diagnostic.** Every analysis ends in a concrete 2-week roadmap
  with named skills and resources, not a vague "improve your resume" verdict.
- **Fast and frictionless.** Paste-and-go, no account-heavy onboarding, results in
  seconds — built for repeated use across many job applications, not a one-time report.
- **Built by someone who lived the problem.** Not a generic B2B SaaS guess — designed
  around the actual experience of applying to dozens of roles as a fresher.

## 5. Future improvements

- Resume upload (PDF/DOCX) with automatic text extraction, instead of copy-paste only.
- Paste a job **URL** and auto-fetch the JD instead of pasting text.
- Track roadmap progress over time and re-score after the candidate reports completing
  milestones.
- Placement-cell dashboard: bulk upload a batch of student resumes against a target JD or
  role, get an aggregate "batch readiness" report.
- Integration with real course catalogs (Coursera, freeCodeCamp, YouTube) so "resources"
  in the roadmap are clickable, verified links instead of AI-generated suggestions.
- Interview-question generator based on the specific gaps identified.
- Employer-side mode: recruiters paste a JD once and screen multiple candidate resumes
  against it for a quick shortlist.
